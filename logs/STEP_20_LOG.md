## 3. Implementation summary

* **Domain (`Ebm.Domain/Scouting`):** `ScoutingAssignment` (focus Player/Region/League/Position/Youth, filters,
  durations), `ScoutReport` (estimated attributes, overall, potential, margins, value, salary), `ScoutingRules`
  (visibility, accuracy, margins, repeat bonus, scout numbers and ability, stable seeds), `ScoutingEstimator`
  (bounded triangular errors); `StaffMember.Ability`.
* **Services:** `IScoutingService` / `ScoutingService` (scouts, assign, cancel, assignments, report search and
  history, hiring of missing scouts, daily processing); `ScoutingKnowledge` (reports or background knowledge for AI
  recruitment); `CareerService` hires scouts at career start and processes scouting every game day
  (`GameDayReport.Scouting`); `TransferService` AI window judges candidates by the club's knowledge.
* **Persistence:** migration `20261117124356_Scouting` (add-column and create-table only).
* **Desktop:** *Scouting* tab (`ScoutingViewModel`, `Themes/Scouting.xaml`); competitions lookup.
* **Step 19 fixes found by the first full integration run:** the registration deadline exemption used
  `SquadRules.Default` (min 10) even when the competitions set a lower minimum (`ClubSeason.RegistrationAsync`);
  `EuropeanTransferWindowTests` assumed free agents in the European dataset (it has none): the attractiveness test now
  prices a contracted player, the window test replenishes free agents first, as a window day does, and every activity
  assertion reports a summary.

## 6. Known limitations
- Database screens (profile, market, squad) still show true ratings; only the scouting screen and AI recruitment use estimates.
- Scouts are generated by the game (datasets have no staff); no hiring screen, specialities or experience.
- Reports are snapshots and do not follow training or ageing.
- The AI transfer window on the European dataset is slow (many minutes; market reloaded per club). Pre-existing.
